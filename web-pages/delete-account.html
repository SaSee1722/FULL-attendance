Account & Data Deletion Request

Last updated: April 24, 2026

You have the right to request deletion of your AttendX account and all associated personal data. Please follow the steps below to submit your request. We will process it within 30 days.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Compose an email using the button below, or manually send an email to salabadeshwaran@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: "AttendX – Account Deletion Request".
  3. In the body, include: your registered email address and your full name as used in the app.
  4. Send the email. You will receive a confirmation response within 5 business days, and your account and data will be fully deleted within 30 days.
✉️ Send Deletion Request Email
⚠️ Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, your attendance records, profile, and all associated data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What Data Gets Deleted

Data Type Action Retention Period
Account credentials (email, password hash) Permanently Deleted Immediately upon confirmation
Profile information (name, department, photo) Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Attendance records associated with your account Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Anonymized / aggregated analytics data May Be Retained Retained (cannot be linked back to you)

Partial Data Deletion (Optional)

If you wish to delete only specific data (e.g., profile picture or attendance history) without deleting your entire account, you can specify that in your email request. We will honor reasonable partial deletion requests.

Questions or Concerns? If you have any questions about this process or our data practices, please review our Privacy Policy or contact us at salabadeshwaran@gmail.com.
